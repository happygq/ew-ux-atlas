# G204 v0.3 · Proportional Gable Family — Research Contract

**Status:** candidate pending owner mobile visual review (2026-11-10). **Scope:** independent EW-UX only; no WP1.1/Authority change.

## Intent

The previously demonstrated T/U white stepped walls were mechanically repetitive and overly triangular from a distance. Generate readable Huizhou Fantasy wall-top silhouettes with extended horizontal coping, assigning a controlled family of profiles to architectural roles. Pure continuous white plaster remains the default; `LEGACY_DECORATED` is only a manual comparison.

## Parametric geometry

Coordinate system remains metres, world Y up; local X is the gable cross section, local Z the exterior façade normal. The physical face orientation and stepped-silhouette handedness are separate concepts. Profile output has `plateaus`, `polygon`, `tierCount`, `minTread`, `crestWidth`. All real cap-stone meshes must follow the same `plateaus` contract.

- Effective gable width is `2 * roofHalfSpan + 0.44m` for T/U groups. Profiles may be studied independently at widths 2.5–5.8m.
- `BILATERAL`: under 3.22m => 2 height tiers and 3 cap sections; 3.22–4.55m => 3 tiers and 5 sections; at/above 4.55m => 4 tiers and 7 sections. A broad centered crest (at least 0.86m) and mirrored side steps replace a narrow mechanical apex.
- `HALF_FAN`: below 3.15m => 3 sections; 3.15–4.55m => 4; at/above 4.55m => 5. Visible horizontal sections taper gradually; the low outer stage extends furthest.
- `ASYMMETRIC`: explicitly selected non-centered crest; never silently substituted for the courtyard mirrored pair.
- Thresholds and proportions are **candidate UX aesthetic heuristics**, not claims about historical Huizhou building codes or physical structural capacity.

## Role grammar (`gableStyle = AUTO`)

| Layout | Architectural position | Silhouette | Handedness |
|---|---|---|---|
| T | branch-axis primary wall | BILATERAL | continuous one-body profile |
| T | two secondary main-run end walls | HALF_FAN | opposite-handed pair |
| U | two principal main-run end walls | BILATERAL | same family, single-body walls |
| U | courtyard-facing two wing ends | HALF_FAN | left/right reflected pair |

Use at most two silhouette families in the canonical T/U scene; neighboring walls share tile/stone style, roof pitch and consistent outside-facing semantics. The pattern is deterministic, not randomized per refresh. The UI allows manual all-bilateral, all-half-fan and asymmetric comparisons without changing the default recommendation.

## Boundary and safety invariants

1. One continuous `wall-body` per gable; a bilateral wall is **not** two interpenetrating full wall meshes.
2. The complete staircase and the physically generated cap-stone sections are compared within 1e-5m tolerance. Forged geometry must be rejected.
3. Both U courtyard half-fans mirror physically (walls, tiled coping, finials and any explicitly enabled exterior trim). External-facing semantics remain independent.
4. Real footing reaches Y=0.025m, physical support and the validated outer-facade plane are preserved.
5. For default `PLAIN`, no exposed-masonry, masonry-joints, raised-step-piers or artificial corner reveals are emitted.
6. The roof/valley/drainage and other non-gable scene geometry must retain the accepted G204 v0.2 hash for identical roof inputs. The G203 accepted L-valley implementation stays untouched.
7. Deterministic re-generation, invalid profile rejection, minimum horizontal tread >=0.49m, and intentional tampering rejection are mandatory automated checks.

## Reproducibility / evidence

- `node --test tests/*.test.js` -> **63/63 pass** in the G204 v0.3 isolated source copy; 42 prior G204 v0.2 checks and 21 added profile-family checks, all in the same Node run.
- `node export-previews.mjs` generates real triangle data; `python render-tu.py` renders static RGB previews from those triangles (NumPy, Pillow, OpenCV; not generated concept art).
- Dynamic preview offers T/U switch, gable styles, roof half-span 1.30/1.55/1.70m (the U wide case increases main roof length to maintain valley end clearance), and white/legacy wall comparison.
- Current tested T default: 73,545 triangles. Current tested U default: 96,622 triangles (these are case-specific geometrical budgets, **not** measured device GPU performance).
