# Layered Gable Composition Contract v0.1

Domain: `ew/ux/layered-gable-streetscape/v0.1` (research, not Authority).

**Coordinate space:** metres, X ridge/depth progression, Y up, Z roof cross-span. Floor Y=0.025. Main street-oblique observation from +X/-Z with three explicit depth bands, front/middle/back. Building positions are fixed templates with bounded deterministic dials, not independent random scatter.

**Placement contract:** Each item has a full physical footprint (inc. gable footing + roof eaves); pairwise footprint intersections above 0.025 sqm axis overlap are rejected. Three role bands required; main middle silhouette must be distinguishable by height. Styles must include bilateral and paired half-fan handedness variants. No purely visual detached wall.

**Visual direction:** family coherence + controlled diversity; foreground guides, midground main wall, background rhythm; asymmetry through side-shifts and height variations without chaotic free rotation. More tightly packed village morphology, actual façade visibility fractions and rightsized street corridors are future research. This is *not* historical reconstruction.

**Non-regression:** No edits to published G203/G204 files; T mirror and U diminished wings are an independent candidate. Preserve roof/valley mesh ownership and G203 geometric test witnesses. Independent model-state acceptance only, not blanket product acceptance.
