# G204 v0.2 · Plain-plaster Gable Decision / Visual Gate

**Approved design direction (owner):** reduce the custom gray-brick appliqués and parallel vertical trims that made the gable read as decorated prefab panels. Preserve the clean Huizhou white-wall architecture and stepped slate-capped silhouette.

## Independent model axes

`gableStyle` determines *cross-sectional silhouette* (`AUTO`, `BILATERAL`, `ASYMMETRIC`). `gableSurface` determines *surface detail* (`PLAIN` by default; `LEGACY_DECORATED` as opt-in comparison). The exterior normal and floor datum are separate, previously verified assembly requirements.

## Explicit removal in PLAIN

`exposed-masonry`, `masonry-joints`, `raised-step-piers`, `step-corner-reveals`, and `endwall-corner-reveal` are absent from the output mesh list. Retain the continuous `wall-body`, minimal lower `plaster-trim`, necessary horizontal `raised-cornice-shadows`, stone socle, structural tile coping, ridge and terminal details. Remove redundant decoration geometry from both original asymmetric and new bilateral profile builders.

## Acceptance criteria

1. T center: one continuous symmetrical stepped gable, no twin-wall overlap.
2. U two wings: matched mirrored silhouette; each outside face still faces outward.
3. Default plain wall shows no repeated gray-brick patchwork or decorative vertical strips in T/U orbit views.
4. Before/after comparison toggle preserves identical roof, tile, ridge, valley, drain and foundation meshes.
5. No decorative mesh can be silently injected into PLAIN without contract rejection.
6. Local unit and deterministic tests pass; user must inspect both T/U styles in Safari before G204 v0.2 visual acceptance.

**Exclusion:** room-side pier density is tracked separately in G2/G3. This change does not erase necessary structure, invent indoor finishing, or modify Authority/world rules.
